Embodied AI · Manipulation

WIZARD

Industrial robots need re-teaching for every new task. WIZARD uses one-shot imitation learning, developed with UC Berkeley, to adapt from a single demonstration with zero retraining.

~14×
success rate over the one-shot baseline on our evaluation set
1
video demonstration is all it takes
StageActive research project, not yet in production. Validated on real hardware.

Robotics · Tactile

T-Rex

Dexterous manipulation degrades when contact is uncertain and vision alone cannot resolve it. T-Rex closes the loop between touch and control. A 36-researcher multi-institutional collaboration — our contribution is documented in the author list, not claimed as sole authorship.
