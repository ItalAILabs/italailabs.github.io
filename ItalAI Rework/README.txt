ItalAI — website prototype
Export date: 4 August 2026

HOW TO OPEN
Open "ItalAI Home.dc.html" in a browser (Chrome or Safari). All pages are linked
from the nav and footer, so you can walk the whole site from there.

Note: keep the files together in this folder. The pages load support.js,
image-slot.js and the assets/ folder using relative paths.

REQUIRES AN INTERNET CONNECTION
Three things are loaded from the web rather than bundled here:
  - Fonts (JetBrains Mono, DM Sans, Spectral) from Google Fonts
  - Team photos and the About hero image from italailabs.com
  - Demo videos from the WIZARD and T-Rex public project pages
Offline, the pages still render but those items will be missing. Say the word
if you want a fully self-contained version.
