ORIGIN & STANDING

Research first, by construction.

ItalAI is a spinout of Sapienza University of Rome, founded in 2024 and staffed by researchers who publish in the venues our field is judged by.

We collaborate with leading universities, research centers, and industry partners to advance artificial intelligence, and translate that knowledge into deployable systems. Our work spans fundamental research, applied AI, and system-level design, with a strong emphasis on correctness, reliability, and scalability.

THE NETWORK

One team, two research cultures.

Our co-founder works from the Bay Area. That is not a satellite office — it is how we stay inside the conversation that sets the direction of this field months before it reaches a paper.

Rome gives us researchers who go deep and stay on a problem. The Bay Area gives us line of sight to what is about to matter. Work moves in both directions: a method read early in California gets stress-tested in Rome, and a result from Rome gets shown to the people best placed to break it.

HOW WE WORK

From open question to working system.

Work is organised around milestones with defined outputs, and reported in plain language you can put straight into an internal update.

SEE THE RESEARCH RECORD HOW AN ENGAGEMENT WORKS →
01

FRAME THE PROBLEM

We start by establishing what a successful outcome actually looks like, and whether the problem is research-shaped at all. Sometimes the honest answer is that it is not.

02

BUILD AGAINST A BENCHMARK

Every milestone produces something measurable and an explanation of what it means for the capability being built — not a status report.

03

MOVE IT ONTO HARDWARE

A method that only works in simulation is not finished. We validate on real hardware, in the conditions the system will meet.
