One-shot imitation learning ItalAI × UC Berkeley

WIZARD

Problem
Industrial robots need re-teaching for every new task and every change in the environment.
Approach
One-shot imitation learning, developed in active collaboration with UC Berkeley: the system watches a single demonstration and adapts to new tasks with zero retraining.
Stage
Active research project, not yet in production.
1
demonstration, then autonomy on unseen tasks — no retraining.
~14×
success rate over the one-shot baseline on our evaluation set — benchmark on the project page.

Tactile-reactive manipulation Multi-institutional

T-Rex

Problem
Dexterous manipulation degrades when contact is uncertain and vision alone cannot resolve it.
Approach
Tactile-reactive control fusing touch with vision. A 36-researcher multi-institutional collaboration — ItalAI's contribution is documented in the author list, not claimed as sole authorship.
Stage
Active collaboration, currently in the research and validation stage.
36
researchers across the collaboration — ItalAI among the named affiliations.
2
sensing modalities fused — touch and vision, in one control loop.

What We Build

We build Physical AI systems for hard problems with no off-the-shelf solutions — perception, manipulation and real-time control in environments that break standard tooling.

Every system starts as research: published, benchmarked, and developed alongside the institutions doing the underlying work. That is what it takes to build something that holds up outside the lab.
